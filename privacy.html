Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameTechClips, operated by Joshna Yarlagadda ("we", "us"), handles information in connection with the GameTechClips website and the GameTechClips publishing tool (the "Service").

1. Who uses the Service

The publishing tool is a single-creator tool used only by the GameTechClips account owner to publish the owner's own gaming clips to the owner's own accounts. We do not offer accounts to the public and do not collect data about other TikTok users or members of the public.

2. TikTok data we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok (TikTok Login Kit) and authorizes the app, the Service may access only:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used to confirm the correct account (@gametechclips) is connected before publishing.
  • The ability to upload and publish videos (scopes video.upload and video.publish, Content Posting API): used to upload the owner's own video clips and captions and publish them to the owner's TikTok account at scheduled times.

We do not access followers, likes, comments, direct messages, contacts, or any other users' data.

3. How we use the information

The information above is used only to authenticate the account owner, show which account is connected, and upload and publish the owner's videos. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Third-party integration provider

Posting is performed through a third-party integration provider (Composio). The OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok are stored by that provider only to perform posting on the owner's behalf. Other platforms (YouTube, Instagram, X) are connected the same way.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, except with the integration provider as needed to publish content, or where required by law.

6. Data retention

Basic profile information and access tokens are kept only while the TikTok connection is active. When access is revoked or the connection is removed, the tokens are deleted by the integration provider and no TikTok data is retained by us. Published videos remain on TikTok under the owner's control until the owner deletes them there.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the Service's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or by disconnecting the account in the integration provider.

8. Deletion requests

To request deletion of any data related to the Service, email joshnayarlagadda@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Website, cookies and tracking

This website is static. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers and sets no cookies other than any strictly necessary ones set by our hosting provider (Vercel) to deliver the site securely. The hosting provider may process standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Security

We rely on the security measures of TikTok's OAuth system and our integration and hosting providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: joshnayarlagadda@gmail.com.
